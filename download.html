Choose Your Install

Online or Offline Install

Friday comes in 3 downloads: the installer, the Voice Pack and the Models Pack. Pick the way that suits your internet. Both give you the exact same Friday.

Easiest

Online Install

Download 1 file. The installer fetches the rest for you.

  1. Download 1 · Installer (Friday-Setup-1.0.0.exe).
  2. Double-click it and follow the wizard.
  3. When the wizard reaches the data step, it downloads the Voice Pack and Models Pack by itself. A progress window shows you how far it is.
  4. If your internet drops, run the installer again. Downloads resume where they stopped.
  5. Open Friday from the Start Menu. Done.
Choose this if: your internet is stable and you want the simplest path.
Download Installer
No internet needed to install

Offline Install

Download all 3 files, put them together, run the installer.

  1. Download all three: 1 · Installer, 2 · Voice Pack and 3 · Models Pack.
  2. Put them in the same folder (for example, your Downloads folder, or a USB stick).
  3. Do not unzip the two .zip files. Leave them as they are.
  4. Double-click the installer. It spots the two zip files next to it and installs everything from them, with no downloading.
  5. Open Friday from the Start Menu. Done.
Choose this if: your connection is slow or keeps dropping, you want to install on several PCs, or the PC you are installing on has no internet. Download on another PC and carry the 3 files over.

Updates
Friday checks for updates once a day and asks you Yes or No first. When only the app changes, you do not download the packs again.
Windows warning? If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This shows because the installer is not signed with a paid certificate.
Optional Ollama AI: the wizard has a checkbox to install Ollama and a small offline AI model, so Friday can chat without internet. This part is a download of about 1 GB and needs internet, even in an offline install. Untick the box to skip it.
Only some files in the folder? No problem. Whatever pack is missing is downloaded for you during the install.
No admin needed. Friday installs for your user account only. Windows may ask for permission once if the Visual C++ runtime or the Everything search service must be installed.

Core Highlights
  • Voice controlled: say the wake word, then speak naturally. No need to remember exact wording.
  • Private and offline: speech recognition and Friday's voice run on your PC. Nothing you say is sent away.

Process Flow

How It Works

A streamlined pipeline from voice to action, all running on your machine.

01
You Speak the Command
Friday runs quietly in the background, listening via your microphone. When you say the wake phrase followed by an app name, it captures your voice input as a local audio buffer, nothing is streamed anywhere.
02
Moonshine Decodes Your Speech
The Moonshine offline speech recognition model processes the audio entirely on your CPU. It converts your voice to text in milliseconds using a compact, locally-stored language model trained specifically for command-style speech.
03
Intent Matching
The transcribed text is matched against Fridays's internal command registry, which maps natural-language phrases to your installed applications. Fuzzy matching ensures you don't need to say the exact app name every time.
04
App Launches Instantly
Once a match is found, Friday fires the corresponding Windows process or shell command. The target application opens, no mouse click, no keyboard shortcut, no context switching required.
05
Friday Confirms via Voice
Piper TTS synthesises a spoken confirmation using the Jarvis-matched voice profile. You hear "Opening Chrome" or "Launching Spotify", crisp, cool, and immediate. The whole cycle completes in under one second.

Security & Privacy

Your Voice, Your Machine

Privacy is not an optional feature in Friday, it's the entire architecture.

Zero Surveillance Architecture

Friday was designed from day one around a single principle: your voice data is yours alone. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no usage reporting, and absolutely no transmission of audio or transcripts to any server ever.

  • All speech-to-text runs on your CPU via Moonshine
  • No microphone stream sent to the internet
  • No account, login, or registration required
  • Models stored and run 100% locally
  • Voice synthesis via the local Piper TTS engine
  • No background data collection of any kind
  • Works fully offline after first-use model download
  • Open command logs only on your own device
